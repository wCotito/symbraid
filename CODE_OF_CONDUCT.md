# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

We want Symbraid to be a welcoming, practical project for everyone. We expect
participants to be respectful, constructive, and mindful of different
backgrounds and experience levels.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks,
unwanted sexual attention, publishing private information, and other conduct
that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unable to participate.

Report a concern privately through the process in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
Reports will be handled discreetly, fairly, and without retaliation. Project
maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

This code of conduct is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1.
